Four range rules

Why you will not get stranded.

  • 1Keep it at 65–70 mph. Speed is the only thing that meaningfully eats EV range.
  • 2Run the AC or heat like normal. Climate control barely matters — speed does.
  • 3The dash always shows miles remaining. If it ever reads below 60 miles before a planned stop: slow to 60 mph and call Josh. There are backup chargers along the route.
  • 4The stops are placed so the battery never drops below ~25%. You're never gambling.
